Hortensia Herbalist Tea

Branding design for an upscale artisan small-batch herbal tea company. It is rooted in a deep reverence for natural history, botany, and the timeless traditions of herbal healing. The name Hortensia, speaks to the brand's ethos. Inspired by the Latin word hortus, meaning “garden,” it embodies the art of growing, tending, and understanding the natural world.
