Etchings

Drawing inspiration from the exquisite engravings of antique natural history books, "Etchings" celebrates the enduring beauty of linework across both traditional and digital mediums. Hand-inked illustrations sit alongside vector artwork created in Adobe Illustrator, blending centuries-old artistic traditions with contemporary design. United by a shared appreciation for precision and craftsmanship, these works capture the intricate forms of flora and fauna with a timeless sense of curiosity and wonder.
