what is the society

Wildwood Conservatory is a sanctuary where cultivated beauty and untamed wilderness grow side by side. Across our miles of parkland, manicured gardens give way to protected native habitats, creating a home for flourishing plants, local wildlife, and the people who come to learn from them. We hold a rare collection of heirloom plant varieties alongside an archive library to learn more about botany and entomology that connects generations of study to the work we do today. Part botanical garden, wildlife refuge, and nature preserve, Wildwood exists to nurture a deeper relationship between people and the natural world.

Joining the Wildwood Society is a simple way to make a lasting impact. There are three types of memberships to choose from and each one includes invitations to special Conservatory events, seasonal updates, and opportunities for community and education.

memberships

erigeron society

Enjoy unlimited annual admission for one, member savings, and early access to special events while helping care for the Conservatory's gardens, trails, and natural spaces.

hellborous society

Includes unlimited annual admission for two, 2complimentary guest passes each quarter, invitations to exclusive member events, and admission to four classes or workshops each year.

sequoia society

Premium membership includes unlimited annual admission for up to 4 people, 3 complimentary guest passes each quarter, access to the the Archive Library and members-only spaces, and a 20% discount at the shoppe.
