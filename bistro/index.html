The Restaurant

The Black Goose Bistro offers casual lunch and dinner fare in a relaxed atmosphere. The menu changes regularly to highlight the freshest local ingredients. Whether you are dropping in for a quick bite during a workday or settling in for a cozy evening with friends, our friendly staff is dedicated to making every visit special.

Catering

You have fun. We'll handle the cooking. Black Goose Catering can handle events from snacks for a meetup to elegant corporate fundraisers. We provide full-service support, ensuring that every detail—from presentation to clean-up—is handled seamlessly so you can focus entirely on enjoying time with your guests.
