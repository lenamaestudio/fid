Our Baked Goods


We start our day at the crack of dawn to bake our own muffins, bread, and dinner rolls. Loaves not used that day are donated to the local food shelter.

Our Burgers & Fries


People come from all over to enjoy our lovingly made burgers. We grind our own locally-sourced organic beef and turkey so you know it's fresh and free from fillers and other nonsense. Go for one of our creative topping combos or stick with the classics.


Indulge in our signature gourmet fries, where the humble potato is elevated to an art form. Each batch is double-fried to achieve a shatteringly crisp golden exterior while maintaining a cloud-like, fluffy interior. Tossed in a bespoke blend of seasoning and finished with chef-inspired toppings, ranging from earthy truffle oil and aged parmesan to decadent, savory infusions—these fries are designed to be the ultimate companion to any meal or a snack in their own right.

Catch of the Day


Our chef works with local fisherman to pick the freshest the sea has to offer for our daily seafood special. Our Roast Cod Caponata with Roasted Potatoes is an old favorite with our regulars.

Southern Fried Chicken


Experience the ultimate comfort, perfected. Our gourmet fried chicken features sustainably sourced, premium-cut poultry, marinated for twenty-four hours in a proprietary blend of buttermilk, hand-ground spices, and garden-fresh herbs. Each piece is hand-dredged in our signature seasoned crust and pressure-fried to order.

Healthy Plates


Tabbouleh: elevate your palate with this signature dish that celebrates the essence of fresh, garden-grown ingredients. Tabbouleh is a vibrant Levantine salad defined by a generous base of finely chopped parsley, crisp mint, vine-ripened tomatoes, and nutty bulgur wheat. Each serving is hand-tossed in a bright, zesty fusion of cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil and freshly squeezed lemon juice, creating a symphony of herbaceous and citrus notes.
